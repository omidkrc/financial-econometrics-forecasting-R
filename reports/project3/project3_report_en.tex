\subsection{Objective}\label{objective}

This project uses two Tehran Stock Exchange industry indices: the
leather-products index and the petroleum-products index. The data were
downloaded with the TSE client and cleaned in Excel. The report merges
the two daily datasets, converts the frequency to monthly and quarterly
observations, tests for seasonal unit roots using the HEGY procedure,
applies seasonal filters, and estimates SARIMA models for forecasting.

\subsection{HEGY seasonal unit-root
tests}\label{hegy-seasonal-unit-root-tests}

For each quarterly series, the HEGY test is first estimated with an
intercept, a deterministic trend, and seasonal dummies. Since these
deterministic components are not statistically strong, the reduced
specification without deterministic terms is also reported.

For the leather-products index, the reduced HEGY statistics are
approximately \texttt{tpi\_1\ =\ 0.820}, \texttt{tpi\_2\ =\ -1.886}, and
\texttt{Fpi\_3:4\ =\ 2.814}. At the 5 percent level, these statistics do
not provide enough evidence to reject the seasonal unit-root nulls. The
series therefore shows evidence of quarterly, semiannual, and annual
seasonal unit-root behavior.

For the petroleum-products index, the reduced HEGY statistics are
approximately \texttt{tpi\_1\ =\ 1.952}, \texttt{tpi\_2\ =\ 2.511}, and
\texttt{Fpi\_3:4\ =\ 0.435}, again indicating that the seasonal
unit-root nulls are not rejected in the unfiltered series.

\subsection{Seasonal filters}\label{seasonal-filters}

the relevant unit-root components. The leather index becomes seasonally
stationary after seven filtering steps. The petroleum index becomes
seasonally stationary after five filtering steps. The additional
nonseasonal differencing order is \texttt{d\ =\ 0} for both filtered
series.
